        The rules

        These are the final rules. There shall be no changes.

        Gift limit
        $20
        Who plays
        1. Everyone brings one wrapped gift for the pile.
        2. Everyone draws a number. That's your turn order.
        3. Player 1 opens any gift from the pile.
        4. Each player after that either steals any opened gift or opens a new one.
        5. If your gift is stolen, you immediately steal or open again (but you can't steal straight back).
        6. A gift can be stolen three times. After the third steal it's frozen and stays put.
        7. Once every gift is open, Player 1 gets one last chance to steal any unfrozen gift.
